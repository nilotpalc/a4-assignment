# C4. Vendor safeguards and integrated go / no-go (9 marks)

**Table 1. Minimum safeguards for the external dependencies of a narrow in-house build**

The build is in-house (C3), so there is no new vendor contract. The external dependencies are the frontier models rented through the organisation's Microsoft Azure partnership, reached through the AI CoE model gateway (Ref E14), and the licensed subscribed sources. The organisation's agreement has not been reviewed for this assignment, so every requirement below is a proposed minimum to confirm in the existing agreement or to add to it (Hypothetical). The same terms would apply to a specialist if the C3 reversal to Partner is triggered.

| AI-specific safeguard taught in Course 4 | Minimum requirement / evidence to request | Why it matters for this initiative |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Data rights and permitted model training / retention / subprocessing | No training on prompts, extracts or outputs; zero retention beyond processing, including any provider-side monitoring or logging copies; EU-region processing; named sub-processors with notice of change. Evidence: the data-processing clauses, the current sub-processor list, and a gateway configuration export showing region and retention settings, supplied by the Head of AI CoE and read by Legal counsel. | C2 control 2 rests on these terms, and they are assumed, not seen (Ref E14). Prompts carry unpublished price-sensitive results (Ref E13); a retained copy is B4 incident type (b). |
| 2. Intellectual-property risk, indemnity and liability allocation | Provider indemnity for third-party IP claims on outputs, confirmed to cover use through the gateway. Liability for confidentiality breach or data misuse not capped at fees paid. The organisation owns prompts, packs, connectors and the evaluation harness. The subscribed-source agreements are understood to allow use of their content in internal AI tools (Org evidence, unverified); the terms of each allowlisted licence must be reviewed and each vendor's confirmation sought (VALIDATE with Legal counsel). Zero retention (safeguard 1) also keeps licensed content out of the provider's stores. Evidence: indemnity and liability clauses; licence terms checked by Legal counsel; written confirmation from each subscribed-source vendor. | Packs quote licensed sources and go to the LT. First-year inference is about USD 8k (Estimate, Ref E5), so a fee-based cap would be trivial against the harm of a leak of unpublished results. |
| 3. Performance SLAs beyond uptime: output quality, drift and remedies | The provider will not warrant the quality of this workflow, so the quality bar is internal: supported-claim rate at least 95% on 20 sampled claims and every figure reproducible (B3). Requirement on the provider: advance written notice of model retirement or version change, and version pinning where offered. Remedy: any model change triggers the B3 re-test before release; below 95%, releases pause and the manual process resumes. Evidence: provider model-lifecycle policy; gateway change log; evaluation-harness results per model version. | The key error is a confident claim its source does not support (B3). Uptime says the model is on, not that it is right; a silent model change is the likeliest cause of drift here. |
| 4. Audit, assurance, incident support and compliance evidence access | Current independent assurance reports for the hosting service; written confirmation that the no-training and retention terms reach through to the model developer behind the cloud service; provider notice of any incident affecting the organisation's data within 24 hours, with logs (Hypothetical). Gateway logs open to the validation reviewer, IT security and the Enterprise Risk Support team. Evidence: assurance reports; incident-support clause; one log-access test before go-live. | Accountability stays with the organisation (B1). The CERT-In 6-hour clock (Ref E9, conditional) and the B4 playbook need provider facts fast; the third-line review before gate 4 needs logs. |
| 5. Exit / portability / deletion / continuity arrangements | Prompts, workflow, connectors, evaluation harness and logs stay in the organisation's tenant in provider-neutral form. Written deletion confirmation at termination; 90 days of transition support. Portability test at gate 2: one pilot pack re-run on a second model through the gateway, scored on the same harness. The Microsoft Azure agreement is understood to allow several models on the gateway (Org evidence, unverified); availability and fit of the second model are validated with the provider at gate 2 (Hypothetical). Continuity: manual process, about 25 analyst-days a pack (B4). Evidence: deletion and transition clauses; portability-test result. | "Build, narrowly" rents the model (C3), so the model is the one part the organisation does not control. A tested switch keeps the C3 reversal real and the monthly LT pack running. |
